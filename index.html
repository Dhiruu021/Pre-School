About Our Institution

A Heritage of Trust, A Future of Brilliance

Founded in 1980 in Bhatpar Rani, Deoria, Bhisham Singh Inter College has shaped multiple generations of successful citizens. Our specialized Bs Global Academy brings the best global early childhood practices to our local community.

We started with a noble dream: to make world-class foundational education accessible to children of Deoria and surrounding regions. Today, our Bs Global Academy blends traditional values of respect and discipline with progressive activity-based pedagogies.

  • Certified Montessori & Kindergarten trained faculty
  • Low student-to-teacher ratio ensuring individual attention
  • Smooth progression into primary and high school wings

Children are natural investigators. We believe real learning happens not through memorization, but through touching, feeling, questioning, and experimenting in a cheerful environment that celebrates mistakes as stepping stones to wisdom.

  • Hands-on sensory exploration
  • Emotional literacy and social bonding
  • Celebrating each child's individual pace

Our mission is to nurture confident, compassionate, and curious young learners with strong linguistic, cognitive, and social foundations, ready to thrive in an interconnected modern world.

  • Character building and value-based upbringing
  • English communication & vernacular respect
  • Continuous parent-school collaboration

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about admissions, safety, meals, or school timings? Find clear answers below.

Children must be at least 18 months old for our Toddler Playgroup. For Nursery / Preschool, the child should be 2.5 to 3.5 years old by March 31st of the academic year. You can also test your child's exact class with our Eligibility Calculator above.

Toddler batch runs from 8:30 AM to 11:30 AM. Nursery runs from 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM, and Pre-K / UKG runs from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We also offer extended care and after-school activity clubs up to 3:00 PM upon request.

Yes, we have dedicated, GPS-tracked school vans with trained female attendants covering Pipra Baghel, Bhatpar Rani, and adjacent routes in Deoria district for safe pickup and drop.

Our entire campus is monitored by high-definition CCTV cameras. Classrooms are sanitized twice daily. All toys and learning apparatus are made of non-toxic, child-safe materials, and only authorized family members with ID cards can pick up children.

You will need: (1) Copy of Child's Birth Certificate, (2) 4 passport size photos of the child, (3) 2 passport photos of each parent, (4) Copy of Aadhaar card of parent/child, and (5) Immunization record copy.
